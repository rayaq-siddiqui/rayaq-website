LeetCode patterns

Every algorithmic idea you need for a coding interview, one pattern per page: the signals that point to it, why it works, a traced example, a Python template, and the Blind 75, Grind 169 and NeetCode 150 problems it unlocks.

Interview problems look endless, but almost all of them are a few dozen patterns in disguise. The skill isn't memorizing solutions. It's reading a problem, noticing the signal ("sorted input and a target sum", "the best contiguous range", "every way to choose"), and reaching for the template that signal implies. Each page here teaches one pattern well enough to rebuild it from scratch, then lists the problems where it is the key idea.

Every page follows the same order: the problem shapes that signal the pattern, the intuition, the mechanics and why they are correct, a worked example traced step by step, an implementation template with variations, its complexity and tradeoffs, the problems it solves, its connections, a short check yourself quiz and references. Pages are marked intro, core or advanced. Pages still being written show as plain text.
